Hi! I am a fifth year Ph.D. student in the Department of Earth and Planetary Sciences at the [University of Hawaii at Manoa](https://www.soest.hawaii.edu/earthsciences/). My research is associated with the [Hawaii Institute of Geophysics and Planetology (HIGP)](https://www.higp.hawaii.edu/), where I am advised by [Dr. Shuai Li](http://www2.hawaii.edu/~shuaili/Home.html).  

I am interested in the origin and evolutio of ice on otherwise rocky airless bodies in our Solar System, such as the Moon and Ceres, and how they interact with physical processes such as impact cratering. I engage in a combination of remote sensning, numerical modeling, and laboratory work.

I am a graduate of [Swarthmore College](https://www.swarthmore.edu), where I completed a B.A. in Astrophysics and a B.S. in Engineering. I am excited to be beginning my planetary science journey! I envision this website to serve as a showcase of my research for interested parties, as well as provide a place for me to document my path through graduate school, and the skills I am developing along the way.

If you have any questions, comments, or suggestions about my research, or just want to say hi, please feel free to reach out via. the links on the left!
